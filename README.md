# Canto Town

Walk around Hong Kong and learn its Cantonese names: a pixel open world (each tile is 30 m of the real
city), starting at Tsing Yi. Ride the MTR between stations you know, open the Town Map, and walk into
**機舖 the Canto Arcade** by Tsing Yi station for the learning games:

- **香港通 Hēunggóng Tūng** — lessons on Hong Kong's places: districts, MTR stations, sights, churches.
- **數字 Soujih · Canto Numbers** — hear a number or see it, then type it.

Names are written in Yale (as in the CUHK CLA textbooks) or, if you prefer, Jyutping (拼音 in the arcade or
in a game's settings). On phones, answers are typed on the game's own keyboard.

Play: https://chapagetti852.github.io/canto-town/ · arrow keys / WASD, the on-screen pad or tap to walk;
Enter, Space or tap to go in. Add it to your home screen: it works offline after one visit.

Built with Phaser 4. Fonts: Fusion Pixel, Silkscreen, VT323 (SIL OFL). Map data: Hong Kong Lands Department,
OpenStreetMap contributors. Photos: Wikimedia Commons (credited in the game).
